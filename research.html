Genealogy becomes manageable when you focus on one person, one record, and one small discovery at a time.

Your guided research path

1

Choose a person

Write down their full name, an estimated year, and the place you associate with them. Start with what your family already knows.

2

Choose one question

Try “Where was she born?” or “Who were his parents?” A focused question makes it easier to recognize a useful record.

3

Compare the evidence

Save the record, note where it came from, and compare dates and relationships before changing your tree.

Before you update your tree

Use this quick record check.

  1. Does the name match, including maiden names, initials, or spelling variations?
  2. Does the age fit the date and the event?
  3. Does the place make sense for this person’s life?
  4. Can you record the source so someone else can follow your work?
